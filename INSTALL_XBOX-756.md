# Twilight Princess Randomizer 1.4.1.756 for Xbox

Install the x64 MSIX over the standard .755 build. Use the included Microsoft.VCLibs.x64.14.00.appx if Device Portal requests a dependency.

Open Settings → Video → World Lighting and turn on Enable World Lighting. The master switch defaults to Off. Vanilla and Randomizer share the same controls. Each effect has an intensity from 0 to 100. Lighting Resolution starts at 540 pixels high and can be set between 180 and 1080. The scene and UI retain their selected resolution.

The lantern and active stage lights supply positions and colors independent of the camera. Nearby visible geometry supplies indirect color and short contact shadows. Reflections trace visible scenery on nearby collision-confirmed water and rain-wet upward faces. Reflections fade at screen edges and can miss transparent or off-screen objects. The fog and sun shafts follow the game's atmosphere and sun. Filmic tone mapping blends over the world before the HUD is drawn.

For a first comparison, hold the camera steady in a lantern-lit room and toggle the master switch. Compare outdoor shadows and sun shafts at different times of day, rain on pavement, and water close to a shoreline. Check the FPS counter at your normal internal resolution, especially with VBAO or Cinematic Depth of Field enabled. Physical Series X performance and visual testing are pending.
